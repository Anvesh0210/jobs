How it works

Not another record system. A coordination layer on the ones you trust.

Every layer is designed for clarity, provenance and safe integration with existing GP, hospital, laboratory and pharmacy systems.

  • Integration gateway

    Validates and maps data from approved GP, hospital, laboratory and pharmacy systems, with idempotent, replayable ingestion.

  • Canonical care model

    Events, episodes and actions modelled on FHIR resources such as Task, ServiceRequest and MedicationStatement.

  • Provenance engine

    Every assertion keeps its source, author, version and received time, so you can always ask "where did this come from?"

  • Action engine

    Explicit states, owners and due dates. Patient-reported and source-verified status are always kept apart.

  • Patient and provider apps

    Accessible web apps with role- and relationship-based access, built for 320px phones up to clinic desktops.

  • Audit and governance

    Append-only audit of every access and change, with clinical safety and privacy governance designed in.

Security and privacy

Built to protect what matters

Safety, security and privacy are properties of the system, not disclaimers. These are the principles the product is designed to.

  • Least-privilege access

    Role and relationship checks on every request, with short-lived sessions and strong professional sign-in.

  • Encrypted everywhere

    Encryption in transit and at rest with managed key rotation, plus tenant and organisation isolation.

  • You decide who sees what

    Connected organisations are always visible to you, and consent can be reviewed and revoked.

  • Append-only audit

    Every view and change is recorded in a tamper-evident log under approved governance.

  • Clinical safety by design

    No diagnosis, dosing advice or result interpretation. A safety case and hazard log guide every release.

  • Data minimisation

    No health data in analytics, logs or crash reports. Only what care coordination needs, for as long as it needs it.

FAQ

Questions, answered

Is HEALNEXIA a medical record?

No. It's a coordination layer on top of the systems your providers already use. Those systems stay authoritative, and every item in HEALNEXIA links back to its source.

Does it give medical advice or interpret results?

No. HEALNEXIA helps you understand and coordinate your care. It never diagnoses, prescribes or interprets results; labels on results come only from the source provider.

Can I use it in an emergency?

Never. Messages are asynchronous and not monitored for emergencies. For urgent help, contact your local emergency number (for example 999, 112 or 911).

What if some of my information is missing?

HEALNEXIA shows which organisations are connected and how fresh each one is. Missing or stale sources are flagged clearly, so an incomplete view is never presented as complete.

Does the prototype use real data?

No. Every person, organisation and record in the prototype is fictional, and it's not connected to any healthcare system. Your clicks are stored only in your own browser.
